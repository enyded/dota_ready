How it works

  1. 1

    Install the desktop agent

    Runs quietly in the background while you queue. No border, no overlay, no interference with DX11 or Vulkan.

  2. 2

    Pair your Companion

    Scan a QR code from the ReAccept app once, or open the Telegram link shown in the same spot on the desktop.

  3. 3

    Get notified

    A ready check appears — your phone buzzes with a push notification immediately.

  4. 4

    Accept from anywhere

    Tap Accept in the notification or the app. Your PC clicks the match for you.

Tapping Cancel in the app only dismisses that notification on your phone — it does not decline or cancel the actual Dota 2 match. If you don't act, the ready check just runs its normal course in-game.

The same flow also covers party invites and launching Dota 2 itself — not just ready checks.

Make sure it works in the background

Android aggressively kills background apps by default. For notifications to arrive reliably, exempt ReAccept from battery optimization and allow background activity in your phone's settings. This takes 30 seconds and is required, not optional.

Privacy

ReAccept only looks at your Dota 2 window to detect the ready-check screen. It doesn't read your screen otherwise, doesn't collect gameplay data, and doesn't share data with third parties. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Will this get me banned?

ReAccept doesn't read game state, doesn't automate gameplay, and doesn't touch memory or the game process. It only detects the ready-check screen from a screenshot and sends a single click — the same thing you'd do yourself. We can't make guarantees about anti-cheat policy, but this is not gameplay automation.

Does it work if my Dota client isn't in English?

Yes. Ready-check detection is locale-independent and doesn't rely on reading any text.

What happens if I'm already at my PC?

Nothing changes — ReAccept doesn't take over if you're present. It only clicks when the remote accept command actually needs to run.

Will it work if my phone is locked or the app is closed?

Yes, as long as background activity is allowed (see the setup step above). The notification arrives from the background, and accepting from it doesn't require opening the app.

Does dismissing the notification or tapping Cancel decline the match?

No. Swiping away the notification and tapping Cancel in the app only dismiss that notification on your phone — they don't send anything to Dota 2 or cancel the actual ready check. The match itself is unaffected either way.

What's free vs. paid?

Notifications are always free, for every match. You also get 3 remote accepts free, total. After that, you'll still get the notification, but accepting remotely requires a subscription.

Do I need the Android app, or can I use Telegram instead?

Either works. Link your Telegram account once from the desktop app and you'll get the same match, party-invite, and remote-launch actions as buttons right in the chat with the bot — no separate app install required. You can also use both at the same time.

Can I unpair my phone or Telegram later?

Yes. Pairing is per-device — scanning the QR again or re-linking Telegram doesn't affect any other device — and can be revoked at any time from the desktop app.

Does this only work for ready checks?

No — the same remote notification-and-action flow also covers party invites (accept or decline from your phone) and launching Dota 2 itself, not just accepting a found match.
